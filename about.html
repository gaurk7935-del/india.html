Ice cream is a beloved, creamy frozen dessert made from dairy milk, cream, sugar, and various flavorings, such as vanilla, chocolate, or fruit. It is famously popular during hot summers for its refreshing, cool texture. Originating as a royal delicacy in ancient times, it is now a widely consumed treat, often served in cones or bowls, that offers a delicious, sweet experience.
